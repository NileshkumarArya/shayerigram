# ShayeriGram Community Guidelines

**Effective date:** April 13, 2026

ShayeriGram is for creative expression, respectful conversation, and safe community participation.

## Allowed
- Original poetry and creative writing
- Respectful feedback and discussion
- Inspiration, emotions, art, and cultural expression

## Not allowed
### 1. Hate and harassment
No targeted abuse, degrading slurs, bullying, or attacks against protected groups or individuals.

### 2. Violent threats
No threats, glorification of terrorism, instructions for violent harm, or celebration of serious harm.

### 3. Self-harm promotion
Do not encourage suicide, self-injury, or eating-disorder behavior.

### 4. Sexual exploitation
No non-consensual sexual content, sexual exploitation, extortion, or any sexual content involving minors.

### 5. Spam and manipulation
No fake likes/follows, mass messaging, scam offers, phishing, misleading links, or engagement manipulation.

### 6. Impersonation and deception
Do not pretend to be another person, brand, public figure, or official entity in a misleading way.

### 7. Privacy violations
Do not post private phone numbers, addresses, passwords, personal IDs, or intimate content without consent.

### 8. Copyright abuse
Do not repost poems, books, lyrics, or images you do not have permission to share.

## Enforcement
Depending on severity or repeat behavior, actions may include:
- Warning
- Content removal
- Reduced visibility
- Temporary feature restriction
- Account suspension
- Permanent ban
