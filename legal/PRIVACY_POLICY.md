# ShayeriGram Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ShayeriGram collects, how it is used, and what choices users have.

## 1. Information we collect
- Account details such as username, email, password hash, and profile details
- Content you upload, including poetry posts, comments, profile image, and saved content
- Basic usage and security data such as IP address, device/browser details, login attempts, and abuse-prevention signals
- Cookies or similar technologies required for login and security

## 2. How we use information
- To create and manage your account
- To show your profile and content to other users according to product settings
- To secure the platform and detect spam, fraud, or abuse
- To improve reliability, performance, and moderation workflows
- To communicate important service and safety updates

## 3. Legal and safety uses
We may preserve, review, or disclose information where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, safety, fraud prevention, platform abuse investigation, or protection of rights.

## 4. Sharing
We do not sell user personal information. We may share data with hosting, database, media-storage, analytics, and security service providers that help operate the platform under appropriate safeguards.

## 5. Cookies
ShayeriGram uses cookies for authentication, session continuity, security, and core site functionality.

## 6. Data retention
We keep information only as long as needed for account operation, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and abuse prevention.

## 7. User controls
Users should be able to update profile fields, remove content, and request account deletion when that flow is implemented.

## 8. Security
We use technical and organizational safeguards, but no service can promise absolute security.

## 9. Children's safety
Users must meet the minimum age required by applicable law. Content involving child sexual abuse or exploitation is strictly prohibited and will be removed and escalated where required.

Before launch, add a real privacy contact email and business/contact address if required by your jurisdiction.
